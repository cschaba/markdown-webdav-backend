Capitals do not matter. The percentage says how well a result matches: 100% is as good as a note named exactly what you searched for. On its own, a word in the title gives about 70%, a tag 30%, a property 25%, a heading 20%, and mentions in the text 5–13%, depending on how often; they add up, so a note that has the word in its title, as a tag and in its text reaches 100% too. It is not relative to the other results, so a search whose best hit shows 5% found nothing that is really about your words. Of the properties the values are searched, not their names. Excalidraw drawings are not searched; attachments are found by file name.
